## ✦ ABOUT STAYFIND ✦

**Stayfind** is a stay and accommodation discovery project designed around a simple browsing experience.

The goal is to make exploring accommodation options feel clear and convenient, with a clean interface that keeps the focus on discovering places to stay.

<div align="center">
<img src="assets/stayfind-features.gif" width="94%" alt="Stayfind animated project highlights"/>
</div>

## 🌙 Project Highlights

- 🏡 **Stay discovery** — a focused experience for exploring accommodation options.
- ✨ **Clean interface** — designed to keep browsing straightforward.
- 🔎 **Easy exploration** — centered around finding suitable places to stay.
- 📱 **Web-based experience** — accessible through a browser.
